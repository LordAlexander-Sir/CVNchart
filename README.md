# Chart of the Settled Sphere

An interactive chart of a fictional interstellar trade network, drawn on the real
star field: 200 settled systems within 300 light years of Sol, positions taken from
the HYG catalogue, six convoy roads, forty-four garrisoned rest posts, thirty farms,
eight Office stations and six years of scheduled crossings.

Everything on it is computed rather than asserted — the jump rule is ten light years,
three days running and one cooling, and every route, garrison, crossing and delivery
time in the chart follows from that.

**[Open the chart](https://NATHAN.github.io/REPO/)** — replace with your own address
once Pages is running.

Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. The tools line under the title measures
distances (`rule`), shows what a convoy can reach (`reach`), and routes a crate from
one world to another through the published timetable (`cargo`).

One file, no build step, no dependencies beyond the three web fonts.
