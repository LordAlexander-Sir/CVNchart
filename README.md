# Chart of the Settled Sphere

An interactive chart of a fictional interstellar trade network, drawn on the real
star field: 200 settled systems within 300 light years of Sol, positions taken from
the HYG catalogue, six convoy roads, fifty-five garrisoned rest posts, thirty farms,
eight Office stations and six years of scheduled crossings.

Everything on it is computed rather than asserted — the jump rule is ten light years,
three days running and one cooling, and every route, garrison, crossing and delivery
time in the chart follows from that.

**[Open the chart](https://lordalexander-sir.github.io/CVNchart/)**

Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. The tools line under the title measures
distances (`rule`), shows what a convoy can reach (`reach`), and routes a crate from
one world to another through the published timetable (`cargo`).

## The four pages

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [The Chart](https://lordalexander-sir.github.io/CVNchart/) | the star field, the roads, the posts and the crossings, in three dimensions |
| [The great convoys' year](https://lordalexander-sir.github.io/CVNchart/convoy-year.html) | the twelve trunk convoys' permanent year: worlds they lie alongside, posts and farms they put in at, crossings they hold, and every near pass |
| [Crossings](https://lordalexander-sir.github.io/CVNchart/convoy-crossings.html) | how the twelve space their meetings through the year |
| [Trade](https://lordalexander-sir.github.io/CVNchart/convoy-trade.html) | what each convoy is worth: the people it puts a hull in front of |

Every page carries a strip of links to the others, so any one of them is a way in.

No build step and no dependencies beyond the three web fonts.
