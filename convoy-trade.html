Read off inputs-v4.json by the same model that draws the chart of the Settled Sphere. Served a year is the population at a convoy's ports multiplied by its laps a year, so a short circuit counts twice. Serves alone is the population at ports no other hull in the fleet calls at — the worlds that would go dark if this one stopped. A doubled road already carries a second hull running the opposite way.
