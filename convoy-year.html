The great convoys' year

Twelve hulls, eleven core worlds each, and a calendar that never changes. The sailing days are permanent, so this is not one year out of six — it is the year, and it was the same year a century ago. Each convoy carries three tracks: the worlds it lies alongside, the posts and farms it puts in at, and the crossings it holds. Drag the day below to find where the whole trunk is on any date.
